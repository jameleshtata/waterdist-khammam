In short

  • This website has no database and no login. What you type stays in your browser.
  • We receive your details only when you choose to send us a WhatsApp message or call us.
  • We use them to confirm, deliver and bill your order. We do not sell them, and we do not send promotions unless you ask.
  • You have legal rights over your data, explained in section 9.

1. Who is responsible

, , (called "we" below), decides why and how your personal data is used for orders placed through this site. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act") this makes us the "Data Fiduciary". You can reach us on .

2. What we collect and how

Details you type on this site (shop or event name, delivery address or area, date and notes, and the bottles you pick). These stay in your browser. They are not sent to any server of ours. They are put into a ready-written WhatsApp message only when you tap the send button, and nothing is sent until you press send inside WhatsApp.

When you message or call us. We receive your phone number, your WhatsApp profile name and whatever you write or say. We receive these because you contacted us.

Personal links. If we gave you a link that already has your shop name in it, the name is part of the web address only. We do not store it.

Technical data. This site has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. We do not set cookies. Your language choice is kept only in your browser for that visit. The company that hosts the site, and the services that supply fonts and YouTube thumbnails and videos (section 6), can see technical details such as your IP address and device type, as any website host can.

3. Why we use your data

  • To confirm price, availability and delivery time with you.
  • To deliver your order and contact you about it.
  • To raise bills, collect payment and keep accounts.
  • To handle a complaint, replacement or refund.
  • To meet legal duties, such as tax records.

We do not use your data for anything else. We do not add you to broadcast lists or send offers unless you ask us to.

4. Your consent

By choosing to send us a message or call us, you agree that we may use the details you give us for the purposes in section 3. You can withdraw this at any time by telling us on WhatsApp or by phone. Withdrawing does not undo what we already did with your data before, and we may then be unable to complete an order that is in progress. We may still keep records the law requires us to keep.

5. Children

This service is for shops, events and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data of anyone under 18. If you think we have, tell us and we will delete it.

6. Who else handles your data

  • WhatsApp (Meta). Messages travel through WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Hosting. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visits.
  • Google. Fonts load from Google Fonts. The Videos page loads thumbnails from YouTube, and a video plays only after you tap it, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced player.
  • Our delivery staff. They see your name, address and phone number only as needed to deliver.
  • Accountants and payment providers. Only if you pay or need an invoice.
  • The brand owner. Only if you complain about a product and ask us to pass it on.
  • Authorities. Only if the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data.

7. How long we keep it

After that we delete or anonymise it, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

8. Keeping it safe

Order chats are kept on our business phone, which is protected by a lock, and only people who need to see an order can see it. This site stores nothing, so there is no site database that could leak. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Your legal rights

Under the DPDP Act, 2023 you have the right to:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you, what we do with it, and who we have shared it with;
  • ask us to correct, complete or update it;
  • ask us to erase it, unless we must keep it by law;
  • have your complaint dealt with, by contacting the person named in section 11 first;
  • name another person to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act yourself;
  • complain to the Data Protection Board of India if our reply does not resolve the matter.

Ask us by WhatsApp, phone or email. We aim to reply within . We may need to confirm who you are before we share or change anything.

10. Consumer rights and legal notice

  • Your consumer rights. Under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to safe goods, to clear information, to be heard and to seek redress. If a product is damaged, leaking, wrong or unsafe, tell us as soon as you can.
  • Outside help. If we cannot settle a complaint you can call the National Consumer Helpline on 1915 or use the consumer commission in your district.
  • Prices and orders. Prices are not listed on this site. An order is not final until we confirm the price, availability and delivery time with you on WhatsApp or phone.
  • Product information. Product names and brand marks belong to their owners and are used only to say what we supply. Nothing on this site is medical advice or a health claim.
